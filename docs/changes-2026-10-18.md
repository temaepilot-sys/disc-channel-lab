# Space sketch update — 2026-10-18

## Rendering and interaction

- Four modes: Particles, Spark Field, Hybrid · Spectral Veil, and Aurora + Spark Field.
- Spark Field combines micro sparks, short streaks and soft floating motes, driven by current channel energy and attacks.
- The veil keeps all frequency bands separate. Band energy moves the corresponding height, while optional upward/downward light flow adds a waterfall-like effect.
- Aurora + Spark Field layers both effects. Choose the original veil palette or match Spark's frequency colors; Spark's bright cores may still look white.
- WASD moves, Q/E turns, Z/X moves vertically. Shift is faster; Alt is finer. Home and Reset camera restore the current mode's initial position and direction. Free movement retains its mode and remembers the preset it departed from, including across saved settings.
- Mute/Solo, Input/Post/Output, LFE visibility, camera/head orientation, pause, seek and saved JSON settings apply to the relevant layers together.

## Reopening and load

The player can reopen a closed viewer window while the companion is still running. A single startup request restores the current view and audio snapshot. A bounded raw-PCM window restores Listener-synchronized Particles when switching from current-audio modes, without waiting for the whole read-ahead period. The limit is 384 packets, 24 MiB and an 8.3-second timestamp span; seek and mixer revisions invalidate old data.

Spark and veil modes reuse the existing FFT. They do not add decoding, sound effects or audio-thread work. The combined mode runs at 30 fps by default and 60 fps when both quality settings are High. GPU programs and buffers are reused across mode and palette changes.

## Builds and checks

Public and personal editions use the same application and viewer sources. `build.ps1` produces `artifacts/public-win-x64` without FFmpeg; `build-personal.ps1` produces `artifacts/personal-win-x64` with the builder's supplied tools and .NET runtime. Keep the entire application folder.

The verification entry point is `tests/verify-space-sketch.ps1`. It covers settings migration, real named-pipe/host behavior, renderer controls, camera navigation, Spark and combined-mode regressions, and shader failure recovery. `-Browser` adds actual Chrome/WebGL checks, including mode-preserving Home, color changes without moving paused particles, reopening, real six-channel PCM and speaker exclusion.

During development, actual WebGL ran on an RTX 3070 Ti at 1080p. Stereo/HRTF queue tests during combined High rendering had no empty buffers. These used a test output sink; they do not prove physical-device latency, listening quality or performance on every GPU. This is an artistic visualization of frequency-band activity, not a measured room sound field.
